Privacy Policy

How Axis AutoConnect handles your data

Axis AutoConnect is built to help Axis College students and verified auto drivers connect safely. We collect only the data needed to create your account, keep your session active, and make feedback useful.

When you sign up, we store your email, hashed password, and role. Feedback submitted through the app is kept to improve your experience and to support ride safety.

We do not sell your information to third parties. Personal data is only shared when necessary to operate the service, comply with the law, or respond to safety incidents involving a ride request.

Cookies and session data are used to keep you logged in and protect your account. We do not use tracking pixels for unrelated advertising.

If you have questions about privacy or wish to remove your account data, contact support@axisautoconnect.com. We are committed to keeping student ride information secure.
